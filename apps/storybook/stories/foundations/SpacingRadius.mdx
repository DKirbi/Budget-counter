# Spacing, radius & sizes

## Spacing — 4 px grid

`p-4` = 16 px on every platform (NativeWind runs with `inlineRem: 16`). The same values are available as
`space(4)` in SCSS and `var(--space-4)` in CSS.

## Control & touch sizes

Every interactive element is **≥ 44 × 44 px** (`size.touchTarget`) except explicitly dense secondary actions (`Button size="sm"`, 36 px).
Operators and travellers both use the app one-handed and in motion.
