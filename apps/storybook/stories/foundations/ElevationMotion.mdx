# Elevation & motion

## Shadows

Used sparingly — borders carry most of the structure. On Android, React Native maps shadows to `elevation`, so the tokens also expose an elevation number in `theme.ts`.

<ShadowScale />

## Motion

Short and functional. Respect the OS "reduce motion" setting: animations shorter than `duration.fast` are skipped, longer ones cross-fade.
